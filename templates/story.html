THE STORY

What makes a good life.

A story of ordinary people, extraordinary choices, and the things we find when we stop looking elsewhere.

THE SYNOPSIS

Some journeys bring us home.

Nil, a middle-aged man, has a good job, good family and even good neighbours, but that doesn't necessarily make him feel good. Surrounded by all that goodness, Nil feels trapped and has to decide whether to continue living like this or change.

THE LOGLINE

Suffocation has become part of a 35-year-old man who feels trapped in what seems to be “a good life”.

01

Camera & light

Nil wakes up. Takes a shower. Picks his clothes for work. Gets dressed. Takes his suitcase. He walks to the dinning room. His wife is doing breakfast. She doesn’t show her face. Nil takes a sit. His wife puts a cup of coffee on the table. Nil doesn’t drink. He stands up and listens to the pet barking. Nil looks at it. His toddler is sitting there too. Nil walks to the door and goes outside. His neighbours say “Have a good day!” altogether. Nil enters his car and drives. He stops driving and walks outside the car. He enters the building where he works. All his colleagues wish him good morning. Nil enters his office.

02

People & performance

The next day, Nil gets to his job. His colleagues wish him good morning. His boss calls him. Nil walks to the main office. His boss announces Nil has been promoted. His boss and colleagues celebrate around him, congratulating him and giving pats on his back. Nil gets home, his wife is waiting with dinner. She calls his friends to celebrate his promotion. The next day, Nil wakes up and gets up. He remains in silence. Stares at the wall. He stands up and grabs the towel, wraps it around his waist. Nil walks to the entrance door. Ignores his wife, his toddler and the pet. Walks outside and ignores his neighbours. He starts to walk faster, almost jogging. He runs. Runs for some time. He stops and lays down. Breathes heavily. Closes his eyes. He slowly smiles.
